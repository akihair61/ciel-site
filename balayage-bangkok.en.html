How long does balayage last?

Because the regrowth line stays soft, balayage lasts longer than a full colour — a touch-up every 3–4 months is typical, depending on hair and brightness.

Do I need bleach? I’m worried about damage.

Brighter designs use bleach, but Ciel selects products and designs the service to keep damage to a minimum. Pairing with a TOKIO treatment is recommended.

The Ciel method — three things we analyse before we touch your hair

At Ciel we do not start from "what style do you want". We start from three analyses, then design the menu that fits you.

  • Your actual hair — thickness, damage, chemical history, natural wave, growth pattern. Checked section by section.
  • Bangkok water and climate — hard tap water, sun, humidity and air-conditioning. We run a mineral-removal prep before every chemical service.
  • Your lifestyle and home care — how you wash, dry and live with your hair, so the result lasts.

Aki (4 years in Japan, 3 in Australia) and View (Thai stylist, Thai-speaking) confirm the plan and the total price before starting.

Balayage, highlights, or full colour — which is right for you

  • Balayage (from ฿5,590 · 4 hrs) — hand-painted, soft from root to end, no hard regrowth line. Best when you want brightness that grows out for 3–4 months. Uses bleach on the painted sections only.

How Ciel designs balayage for Asian hair in Bangkok

Asian hair lifts through orange before it reaches beige, and Bangkok sun pushes lifted hair warm again within weeks. Ciel paints with low-damage lightener, controlled to the exact level each section needs, then tones in a formula that fades to a soft brown rather than orange. Olaplex during and TOKIO after are recommended for every balayage.

  • Consultation with photos: we translate "the Pinterest picture" into what your base can actually reach today
  • Sectioning by how the hair falls when you wear it, not by a grid
  • Freehand painting with soft transitions at the root; foils only where extra lift is needed
  • Toner formulated for the fade-out; home-care briefing (purple shampoo, UV protection)

Can balayage be done without bleach?

Not for true brightness — balayage needs lift. If you want ash or muted tones without bleach, choose the no-bleach double colour (฿2,990). We will tell you honestly which suits your hair.

How often do I need to redo it?

Every 3–4 months for the painted sections; a toner refresh every 6–8 weeks keeps the tone fresh in Bangkok sun.
