Balayage in Bangkok

Phrom Phong · Sukhumvit

Balayage at Ciel Japanese Hair Studio is built on the foil work owner-stylist Aki refined during three years in Australia. A soft, hand-painted gradient from root to tip grows out beautifully with a less obvious regrowth line, so you can stretch the time between appointments. Designed around Bangkok’s strong UV and water, with low-damage bleach and colour planned right through to the fade-out. Consultations in Japanese, English and Thai. About a 2-minute walk from BTS Phrom Phong.

Pricefrom ฿5,590 (varies by length & condition)

How long does balayage last?

Because the regrowth line stays soft, balayage lasts longer than a full colour — a touch-up every 3–4 months is typical, depending on hair and brightness.

Do I need bleach? I’m worried about damage.

Brighter designs use bleach, but Ciel selects products and designs the service to keep damage to a minimum. Pairing with a TOKIO treatment is recommended.
