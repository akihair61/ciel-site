Scalp Treatment in Bangkok

Japanese scalp care · Phrom Phong, Sukhumvit 24
Updated September 2026 · Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki

Bangkok is hard on the scalp: heat and sweat all day, then hours of dry air-conditioning, hard water, sunscreen and styling product that never fully wash out. The result is an oily, itchy or flaky scalp, a heavy feeling at the roots and, over time, finer hair. Ciel's scalp treatment is the Japanese head spa approach: clean the scalp properly, restore circulation, then treat — done by a hair stylist who can also see what your hair needs.

What the scalp treatment does

  • Deep cleanse — a scalp-specific cleanser worked through in sections to lift sebum, sweat residue and product build-up that normal shampoo leaves behind
  • Nano-steam — warm fine mist opens the pores and softens hardened sebum so it releases without scrubbing
  • Pressure-point massage — 15–20 minutes across the scalp, temples, neck and around the eyes, following Japanese head-spa points to restore blood flow
  • Warm water bath — the head rests in flowing warm water; tension in the neck and shoulders lets go and the scalp is rinsed completely
  • Treatment — a scalp serum, and on the 90-minute course the TOKIO 4-step hair treatment for the lengths, then blow-dry

Who it is for

  • Oily scalp / hair flat by afternoon — the most common Bangkok complaint; a monthly deep cleanse changes it noticeably
  • Itching, flakes, tightness — usually build-up and dryness from air-conditioning rather than a skin condition; if we see something that needs a doctor, we will say so
  • Thinning or fine hair — circulation and a clean follicle environment are the base for healthy growth; we are honest that a treatment is care, not a cure
  • Headaches, eye strain, poor sleep — the massage is the part most guests come back for
  • Before colour or straightening — a clean scalp means fewer irritations and better results

Scalp treatment, head spa, head massage — the difference

Head massage (spa or barber) is relaxation only, on dry or lightly wet hair. Scalp treatment at a clinic is often a product applied after a normal shampoo. A Japanese head spa combines both: the cleansing and steam are the treatment, the massage restores circulation, and it ends with a proper hair treatment and blow-dry, so you leave with a styled finish. At Ciel the scalp treatment is the head spa; see the head spa page for the full ritual.

How often

For an oily or itchy scalp in Bangkok's climate, every 3–4 weeks for the first three visits, then every 4–6 weeks for maintenance. Combine with your haircut (60-min course + cut from ฿2,560) so it fits a normal salon rhythm. Between visits: rinse the scalp thoroughly, not just the hair, and keep conditioner off the roots.

Scalp treatment in Bangkok — common questions

How much does a scalp treatment cost in Bangkok at Ciel?

฿2,000 for the 60-minute course and ฿3,000 for the 90-minute premium course with TOKIO treatment. With a haircut, from ฿2,560.

Does it help with an oily scalp?

Yes — this is what most guests book it for. The deep cleanse and steam remove hardened sebum that daily shampoo cannot, and most people notice lighter roots for two to three weeks.

Does it help hair loss or thinning?

It improves the scalp environment and circulation, which supports healthy growth. It is care, not a medical treatment; for sudden or patchy loss we recommend seeing a dermatologist.

Is it the same as a head massage?

No. A head massage is relaxation on dry hair. Our treatment cleanses and steams the scalp first, then massages, then finishes with a hair treatment and blow-dry.

Can men book it?

Yes. Around half of scalp-treatment guests are men, often combined with a cut.

How long does it take?

60 or 90 minutes; add 45–60 minutes for a haircut.

Do I need to wash my hair before?

No. Come as you are — the treatment starts with the cleanse.
