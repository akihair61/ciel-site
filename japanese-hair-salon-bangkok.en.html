Why choose a Japanese salon?

Japanese hairdressing is built on three habits: listening first, cutting with precision so the style still works six weeks later, and protecting the hair — choosing chemistry and technique that leave it healthier, not just brighter. Bangkok's sun, water and humidity are hard on hair, so at Ciel every colour plan is designed through to how it fades, and every bleach service can be paired with TOKIO — the Japanese in-salon treatment famous for restoring strength to damaged hair.

How to choose a Japanese hair salon in Bangkok

Three things worth checking before you book anywhere: who actually holds the scissors (at some salons the Japanese name is marketing — at Ciel, Aki personally takes every guest), real reviews (Ciel holds 5.0 with 214 Google reviews, one of the highest-rated salons in Phrom Phong), and whether the salon can explain your colour in your language — miscommunication is the most common cause of a bad salon day abroad.
