Want a rich ash brown, mocha or smoky tone without bleaching? “No-bleach double colour” is a Japanese two-step technique that is still rare in Thailand. Step one lifts your base with a Japanese lightener colour — no bleach involved. Step two layers your target tone on top. The result: clear, even ash tones close to bleached work, while your hair stays soft and glossy. Ciel's colour team consults for free before every service.

Which tones work without bleach?

Ash brown, mocha, chocolate, olive and mid-to-dark dimensional tones work beautifully without bleach. Very light shades such as pale milk tea or silver still need bleach — we check your actual base and tell you honestly what is achievable, with the closest no-bleach plan if not.

How is it different from a normal single colour?

A single colour deposits one shade over your existing base. Double colour is two steps in one visit: a lightener-colour first to lift the base, then your target tone on top — giving clearer, more even ash results without bleach.

Will my hair get damaged?

No bleach is used, so damage is far lower than bleached work. Pair it with TOKIO treatment on the same day for extra softness and shine.

How no-bleach colour works — and why most salons say "you need bleach"

Asian hair carries a lot of red-orange pigment. Put an ash tone straight on top and it either does not show or turns orange, so most salons bleach first — the most damaging step in hairdressing. Japanese double colour takes another route: step one uses a "lightener colour" (a dye with its own lifting power, not bleach powder) to lift the base 2–3 levels while neutralising orange; step two lays the real tone over it. You get a clear ash result without destroying the cuticle.

Which tones are possible without bleach — the honest list

  • Clearly achievable: ash brown · muted chocolate · mocha · Thai-tea brown · olive · muted red-brown · dimensional darks
  • Soft version: mid–dark milk tea · grey-brown · deep lavender (depends on your base)
  • Still needs bleach: light milk tea · light grey · blonde · pastels · silver — we will tell you straight, and propose Olaplex + TOKIO to limit damage

Process and time

  • Consultation: your current base, colour history, reference photos — and an honest answer on whether it works without bleach

Making ash tones last in Bangkok

  • No washing for the first 48 hours
  • Sulphate-free shampoo, lukewarm to cool water (hot water opens the cuticle and flushes colour)
  • Sun is the enemy of ash — hat or UV spray for long outdoor days
  • Swimming: wet hair with clean water first so less chlorine gets in
  • Re-tone every 6–8 weeks (touch-up ฿2,500) instead of a full service

Does no-bleach colour damage hair?

Far less than bleach, because no powder attacks the cuticle. It is still a chemical service, so we finish with TOKIO and give you home care — hair stays soft and glossy after colouring.

How long does no-bleach colour last?

Ash tones 4–8 weeks depending on washing and sun. We formulate so the fade is a warm brown, not orange, and you can re-tone with a touch-up.

I have bleached before — can I do double colour?

Yes, and it is often easier because the base is already light. We check the damage first and add Olaplex if the hair is fragile.
