Currency Intelligence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Currency Intelligence (“the app”) is published by MIYOSHI BEAUTY GROUP COMPANY LIMITED (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Amounts, conversions, trips, budgets, split-bill entries, alerts and settings you create are saved only on your device (including the app’s widget, which reads the same on-device data). This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. You can export or import it yourself from within the app.

Exchange-rate requests

To show current and historical rates, the app requests public exchange-rate data from third-party providers (Coinbase and Frankfurter). These requests contain only currency codes and dates — no account, name, location or other personal information. Like any internet request, the provider can see your device’s IP address; their own privacy policies apply.

Camera

If you use the price scanner, the camera image is processed on your device to read numbers. Images are never saved or uploaded.

Notifications

Rate alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification token is sent to us.

No tracking, ads or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
