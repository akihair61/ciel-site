Frequently asked questions

Are the rates the same as my bank or exchange counter?
No. The app shows market reference rates with their source and time. Banks, cards and exchange counters add their own spread and fees, so the amount you actually receive will differ.

Does it work offline?
Yes. The last rates you downloaded are kept on your device and clearly marked with the time they were fetched.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Use Export in the app, then Import on the new device.
