The Ciel method — three things we analyse before we touch your hair

At Ciel we do not start from "what style do you want". We start from three analyses, then design the menu that fits you.

  • Your actual hair — thickness, damage, chemical history, natural wave, growth pattern. Checked section by section.
  • Bangkok water and climate — hard tap water, sun, humidity and air-conditioning. We run a mineral-removal prep before every chemical service.
  • Your lifestyle and home care — how you wash, dry and live with your hair, so the result lasts.

Aki (4 years in Japan, 3 in Australia) and View (Thai stylist, Thai-speaking) confirm the plan and the total price before starting.

Highlights vs balayage vs inner colour

  • Highlights (foils, from ฿4,490 half head + toner) — fine, even pieces from the root; the most "polished" brightness and the best way to blend greys. Regrowth is visible but soft.
  • Balayage (from ฿5,590) — hand-painted, brighter at the ends, no root line; grows out longest.
  • Inner / hidden colour (฿3,990) — pieces under the top layer; shows when hair moves or is tied up. Office-safe, lowest commitment.

How Ciel places highlights on Asian hair

Dark hair shows every foil, so placement is everything. Ciel weaves fine pieces where light naturally hits — the top layer, around the face, the fringe line — and keeps the underneath darker for depth. Lift is stopped at the level the toner needs, and the toner is formulated so the fade goes soft brown, not brassy. Olaplex during and TOKIO after are recommended.

  • Consultation with photos: fine or chunky, face-framing or all-over, office-safe or bold
  • Mineral-removal prep for even lift
  • Foils woven by zone; roots last; Olaplex in the bowl
  • Toner for the target and the fade-out; TOKIO to close the cuticle; home care

How often do highlights need a touch-up?

Every 2–3 months for the foils, with a toner refresh every 6–8 weeks to keep the tone fresh in Bangkok sun.

Will highlights damage my hair?

Only the pieces in foil are lightened, and we use Olaplex during and TOKIO after, so overall damage is far lower than a full bleach.
