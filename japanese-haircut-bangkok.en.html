What makes a Japanese haircut different

Many salons cut to match a photo while you sit in the chair; the shape is built with a blow-dry and product, and disappears after your first wash at home. A Japanese cut works the other way round: the shape is designed from your skull and growth direction so the hair styles itself.

  • Consultation before scissors — how short is acceptable, which side your fringe falls, how many minutes you have in the morning, whether you need to tie it up. We agree first.
  • Structure wet, finish dry — the outline is cut wet, but thinning and ends are finished on dry hair, because dry is how you wear it.
  • Internal thinning by zone — Asian hair is thick and straight; uniform surface thinning makes it puff out. We take weight only where it pushes, so it sits flat in humid air.
  • Soft ends, no hard lines — it still looks intentional as it grows. Most guests return every 6–8 weeks.

Styles we cut most often

  • Japanese bob / lob — ends tuck under without a straightener; ideal for thick straight hair and hot weather
  • Long layers with a see-through fringe — light, tie-able, natural
  • Wolf cut / connected fringe and layers — movement for wavy or previously coloured hair
  • Women's short cut — shaped to the nape and head; styles in three minutes
  • Men's cuts — two-block, fades, business styles: see the men's haircut page

Bring a photo. We will tell you honestly how it will look on your hair and what we would adjust for your face shape.

The Ciel method — three things we analyse first

  • Your actual hair — density, coarse or fine, natural wave, cowlicks, growth direction at the nape and hairline
  • Bangkok water and climate — humidity, sun, air-conditioning. A cut that looks right in a cold salon has to survive the walk outside.
  • Lifestyle and maintenance — do you blow-dry, how much, do you tie it for work or the gym

Your stylist explains the plan and the total price before starting. No surprises on the bill.

What happens at your appointment

  • 1. Consultation, 5–10 min — hair check, photos, agree length and fringe
  • 2. Shampoo with a short scalp massage — included
  • 3. Outline cut (wet) → 4. Blow-dry → 5. Thinning and finishing on dry hair
  • 6. A finish you can repeat at home — minimal product, and we show you how to dry it

45–60 minutes. Colour, TOKIO treatment or a head spa can be added to the same visit.

Japanese haircut in Bangkok — common questions

Is a Japanese haircut suitable for non-Asian hair?

Yes. The method is about reading density and growth direction, so it works on fine European hair and curly hair too. We simply thin less and cut curls dry.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Consultations are in English, Japanese or Thai.

How much is a haircut and are there extras?

Ladies' ฿1,200, men's ฿1,000, including shampoo and blow-dry. No length surcharge. Treatments or colour are quoted in full before we start.

Can you cut hair that has been straightened, coloured or bleached?

Yes. We check your chemical history first and adjust the thinning — less on rebonded hair, more care on bleached ends.

How long does it take?

About 45–60 minutes including consultation, shampoo and blow-dry.

Do you take walk-ins?

If a slot is free, yes. Online booking shows live availability 24/7 and lets you choose your stylist.

Where are you?

Ariston Hotel 2F, Soi Sukhumvit 24 — 2 minutes' walk from BTS Phrom Phong, next to Emporium.
