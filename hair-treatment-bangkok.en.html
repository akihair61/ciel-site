Why Bangkok water changes your hair

Bangkok tap water is harder than Japan's; mineral build-up makes hair stiff and colour patchy. We prep the hair to remove build-up first, so treatments and colour work evenly — the reason results here can finally match what you had back home.

The Ciel method — three things we analyse before we touch your hair

At Ciel we do not start from "what style do you want". We start from three analyses, then design the menu that fits you.

  • Your actual hair — thickness, damage from colour or bleach, chemical history, natural wave. Checked section by section from root to end.
  • Bangkok water and climate — hard tap water, sun, humidity and air-conditioning mean hair here needs different formulas and steps than in Japan. We run a mineral-removal prep before every chemical service.
  • Your lifestyle and home care — how often you wash, whether you blow-dry, sport, sun or office air — so the result lasts, not just looks good on day one.

Three treatments at Ciel — how to choose

  • TOKIO Inkarami 4-step (฿1,990 · 1 hr) — builds keratin bonds inside the hair rather than coating the surface. For bleached, frequently coloured or straightened hair. The result is hair that is genuinely stronger; lasts 3–4 weeks, longer with the home-care line.
  • Olaplex (฿1,990 · 15 min) — repairs the disulphide bonds broken by bleach. Done during a bleach or colour service to limit damage while it happens.
  • Kerasilk keratin (฿3,990 · 2 hrs) — anti-frizz smoothing without straightening chemistry; lasts 3–4 months. For frizzy hair that does not want a harsh service.

Why hair dries out faster in Bangkok

Three culprits: hard water (calcium and magnesium deposits make hair stiff, dull and colour uneven), sun and humidity (the cuticle stays open, moisture moves in and out all day, hair swells) and air-conditioning (pulls moisture out for eight hours). A good treatment therefore starts by removing the build-up — otherwise it is cream on top of dust. Ciel runs a mineral-removal prep before every treatment and every chemical service.

The TOKIO process at Ciel

  • Hair check and formula chosen by damage level

  • Steps 1–2: low-molecular keratin driven into the cortex
  • Step 3: INKARAMI locks the keratin into internal bonds
  • Step 4: surface seal, then optional ultrasonic iron (฿500) to push the actives deeper
  • Blow-dry and home-care advice

Treatment or head spa — what is the difference?

A treatment repairs the hair fibre; a head spa cleans and relaxes the scalp. Damaged hair → treatment. Oily, itchy scalp, hair fall or stress → head spa. Both together in the 90-minute Premium head spa, ฿3,000.

How often?
