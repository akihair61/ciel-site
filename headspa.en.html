Head spa · Head massage · Scalp treatment

What is the difference?

Head massage at a Thai massage shop is pressure only — your hair is not washed and you leave with the same scalp you came in with. Scalp treatment at a clinic is product-led and targets oil, dandruff or hair fall, usually without relaxation or styling. A Japanese head spa combines both in one 60–90 minute ritual performed by a hair stylist: a proper double cleanse, nano-steam, pressure-point massage of the scalp, neck and eye area, a warm waterfall head bath, a moisture treatment and a light blow-dry — so you walk out relaxed and with clean, styled hair.

It suits an oily or itchy scalp in Bangkok's humidity, hair-fall worries, tension headaches and eye strain from screen work, hair that feels heavy after colour or bleach, and travellers who want to reset after a long flight. Many guests add it before a colour or a hair treatment, or book it with a men's cut.

Head spa in Bangkok — common questions

What is a Japanese head spa?+

A Japanese head spa is a scalp-focused treatment that combines a deep cleanse, steam, precise pressure-point massage and a warm water bath. Unlike a standard shampoo or a body massage, the goal is to clean the scalp properly, release tension around the head, neck and eyes, and leave the hair soft and light. At Ciel in Phrom Phong, Bangkok, the ritual runs through six steps and finishes with a natural blow-dry.

The 60-minute Ciel Japanese Head Spa with warm water bath is THB 2,000. The 90-minute Premium Head Spa, which adds the TOKIO 4-step repair treatment and ultrasonic iron, is THB 3,000. Final pricing is confirmed in salon as it varies with hair length and volume.

60 minutes for the standard head spa and 90 minutes for the Premium. Many guests book it together with a cut or colour on the same day.

Can men book a head spa?+

Yes. The head spa is popular with male guests, especially for scalp care after long days in Bangkok's heat and humidity, and can be booked on its own without a haircut.

Is a head spa the same as a head massage?+

No. A head massage only applies pressure; a Japanese head spa cleanses the scalp with shampoo and steam, massages the pressure points, rinses with a warm water bath and finishes with a treatment and blow-dry. You leave with clean, styled hair.
