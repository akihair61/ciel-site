Ciel

Japanese Straight Perm

Phrom Phong · Bangkok

In humid Bangkok, straightening to tame frizz and waves is in high demand — but product and technique make all the difference. Ciel uses Japanese products and reads the condition of your hair to adjust the chemistry and iron heat, for a result that is smooth and glossy without going flat or stiff. Fringe-only and partial straightening can be discussed. Japanese, English and Thai spoken, moments from BTS Phrom Phong.

Pricefrom ฿2,990 (varies by length & condition)

Will it look natural?

Yes. We control product and heat to your hair type so it doesn’t go poker-straight or stiff — you keep softness and natural movement.

Can you straighten bleached hair?

To avoid breakage, we may decline straightening on heavily damaged or bleached hair. We always check the condition in consultation first.
