What Japanese straightening is — and how it differs from ordinary rebonding

Japanese straightening restructures the hair with a two-step solution and heat. The chemistry is not the secret; the secret is controlling time, temperature and tension section by section. Most Asian hair is thick and carries several wave patterns on one head, and the roots and ends are never equally damaged. One formula applied everywhere gives you the classic "rebonded" look: flat at the root, stiff at the ends, sometimes breakage.

  • Ordinary rebonding: one formula, whole head, same iron temperature → dead straight but flat, stiff, no movement.
  • Japanese straightening at Ciel: zones with different strength and timing, ironed in thin slices at the lowest effective temperature, roots left with lift, ends curving in naturally.
  • Volume straightening: only the frizzy parts are straightened; roots stay up and ends turn under — for people who do not want poker-straight hair.

The process (about 3 hours)

  • Consultation and strand test — chemical history, elasticity test, agree on the degree of straightness.
  • Mineral-removal prep — Bangkok hard water leaves deposits that block even absorption.
  • Step-1 solution by zone — root, mid-length and ends timed separately, checked every 5 minutes.
  • Rinse, dry, iron — thin slices, temperature matched to the hair, roots spaced for volume.
  • Step-2 neutraliser — locks the new structure; TOKIO treatment the same day is recommended for damaged hair.
  • Home-care briefing — no washing, tying or tucking behind the ears for 48 hours.

Who it suits — and who should wait

  • Suits: frizzy or wavy hair that swells in the rainy season, anyone who irons every morning and wants to wake up done.
  • Suits: thick hair that went flat with ordinary rebonding and wants volume back.

  • Not recommended: re-straightening already-straightened lengths. Touch up the new growth only (฿2,990).

Japanese straightening or keratin — which one?

If your hair is healthy and you want permanently straight hair, choose Japanese straightening. If it has been bleached or is very damaged, or you only want less frizz, choose Kerasilk keratin (฿3,990, lasts 3–4 months, far gentler). We test your hair and tell you honestly before starting.

Should I cut after straightening?

We recommend it: split or damaged ends show more once the hair is smooth. The straightening + cut set at ฿4,671 is cheaper than booking separately.

Can I colour and straighten on the same day?

We normally suggest two weeks apart, straightening first. If it must be the same day, we assess the hair and may switch to a gentler colour.
