Straight Perm vs Keratin Treatment

Phrom Phong · Bangkok

Every week someone asks us for "a keratin to make my hair straight", or for "a Japanese straightening that won't damage my hair". Both requests mix up two completely different services. One is a perm. The other is a treatment. They do different things to the hair, they feel different, and they are chosen for different reasons. This page explains the difference plainly, so you can book the right one.

The short version. A Japanese straight perm makes waves and curls straight, and it is hard on the hair. A Kerasilk keratin treatment smooths frizz and adds shine, and it cannot straighten at all. If you want your waves gone, only the straight perm can do it. If you want your frizz under control while keeping your natural movement, choose the keratin treatment.

1. Japanese straight perm — it is a perm

A Japanese straight perm is a perm. Not a conditioning service, not a care service — a perm, in the same family as a curly perm, only in the opposite direction. In some countries it is sold under names that make it sound like a care service. The name does not change the chemistry. Whatever it is called, it is a perm, and it puts a heavy load on the hair.

This is how it works, in three steps:

  1. First lotion. A reducing agent is applied to break the bonds inside the hair that hold its natural wave in place. At this point the internal structure of the hair is opened up.
  2. Flat iron. While the bonds are open, the hair is pulled straight with a flat iron, section by section. This is where the wave or curl is physically removed.
  3. Second lotion. An oxidising agent re-forms the bonds in the new, straight position and locks the shape in. The straight result is permanent on that hair until it grows out.

Straight-permed hair looks shiny and smooth. It is important to understand why. Straight hair reflects light evenly, so it looks glossy — that look comes from the straight shape, not from the hair being in better condition. Underneath, the hair has been through a strong chemical process and the stress on it is real. This is also why we check every head carefully first, and why we may decline heavily bleached or already damaged hair.

What it is for: wavy, curly or frizzy hair that you want straight. Nothing else does this. If your goal is to remove the wave, a straight perm is the only route.

2. Kerasilk keratin treatment — it is a treatment

A keratin treatment is exactly what the name says: a treatment. Kerasilk contains no ingredient that breaks the bonds inside the hair, and no ingredient that can pull a wave or a curl straight. It does not open up the internal structure of the hair. It works on the surface and the outer layers, smoothing the cuticle, reducing frizz and puffiness, and adding softness and shine.

Because nothing inside the hair is broken or re-formed, the keratin treatment does not straighten at all. A wave stays a wave. A curl stays a curl. What changes is how the hair behaves: it lies flatter, it is less affected by Bangkok's humidity, it dries faster, and it feels smoother to the touch. The effect fades gradually over weeks and washes out; nothing is permanent.

What it is for: hair that is frizzy, puffy or hard to manage, where you want support against the frizz while keeping your natural shape. It is also the gentle choice for coloured or bleached hair that could not take a perm.

Questions we hear most

Is a Japanese straight perm gentle on the hair?

No. A Japanese straight perm is a perm. It breaks the bonds inside the hair with a first lotion, pulls the hair straight with a flat iron, and locks the new shape with a second lotion. Whatever name it is sold under in your country, chemically it is a perm, and it puts real stress on the hair. Looking shiny afterwards does not mean the hair is in better condition.

Can a keratin treatment straighten curly or wavy hair?

No. Kerasilk contains no ingredient that breaks the bonds inside the hair, so it cannot straighten waves or curls at all. It smooths the surface, reduces frizz and volume, and adds shine. The wave stays.

Which should I choose if I want my waves gone?

Only a straight perm can make wavy or curly hair straight. If you want the waves or curls removed, a Japanese straight perm is the only option. If you only want frizz and puffiness under control while keeping your natural movement, choose the Kerasilk keratin treatment.

Why does straight-permed hair look so shiny?

Because the hair is straight, light reflects off it evenly, so it looks shiny and smooth. That look is the result of the straight shape, not of the hair being healthier. The chemical process of a straight perm puts a heavy load on the hair.
