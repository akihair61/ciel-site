High-tone Colour & Bleach

Phrom Phong · Bangkok

Clear, foreigner-style high-tone colour lives or dies on how the bleach is managed. At Ciel, owner-stylist Aki reads the condition of your hair and selects and applies product to keep damage as low as possible. Colour is planned not just for the first day but through the whole fade-out, so it still looks good as it softens. Services in Japanese, English and Thai, moments from BTS Phrom Phong.

Pricefrom ฿2,490 (varies by bleach sessions & length)

How many bleach sessions will I need?

It depends on your target brightness and hair history. In the consultation we suggest a realistic number of sessions and the damage trade-off — we never force excessive one-time bleaching.

My colour fades too fast.

Ciel designs colour with the fade-out in mind, so it still looks good as it softens, and we recommend home care to help it last.
