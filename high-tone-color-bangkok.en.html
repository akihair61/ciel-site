How Ciel bleaches Asian hair with less damage

High-tone colour on Asian hair is a lifting problem: dark, dense pigment has to travel through red and orange before it reaches yellow and pale yellow. Rushing that with strong bleach is what turns hair to straw. Ciel lifts in controlled rounds with lower-volume developer, Olaplex in the mixture and a check every five minutes, and stops at the exact level the target tone needs — not one level more.

  • Milk tea, beige, greige: usually 1–2 bleach rounds to level 8–9, then toner
  • Light grey, silver, platinum: 2–3 rounds to level 9–10, only on hair that passes the elasticity test
  • Pastels and vivid fashion colours: bleach to pale yellow, then semi-permanent pigment
  • "Milk tea without bleach": possible on lighter or previously coloured bases via the no-bleach double colour

The process (3–5 hours)

  • Consultation and strand test; we say clearly how many rounds your hair can take today
  • Mineral-removal prep — Bangkok hard water makes bleach lift unevenly
  • Bleach by zone, roots last (scalp heat lifts faster), Olaplex in the bowl
  • Rinse and assess; second or third round only if the test allows
  • Toner for the target, formulated for the fade (no green, no orange)
  • TOKIO 4-step and ultrasonic iron to close the cuticle; home-care briefing

Keeping high-tone colour alive in Bangkok

  • Purple or blue shampoo once or twice a week, not daily
  • Cool water, sulphate-free shampoo, always a leave-in before heat
  • UV protection outdoors — sun turns lifted hair yellow within weeks
  • Toner refresh every 4–6 weeks (touch-up ฿2,500) keeps the tone without re-bleaching

Can you go platinum in one visit?

Only if your hair passes the elasticity test after the first round. For most Asian hair we plan two visits and say so honestly at the consultation — it is the difference between platinum and breakage.

I was bleached elsewhere and it went orange. Can you fix it?

Usually yes. We assess the level, do a corrective lift only where needed, and tone to neutralise. Bring photos of what was done if you have them.
