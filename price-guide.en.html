2026 edition

Japanese-salon haircuts match Japan

฿1,000–1,500 is the same range as a mid-tier Tokyo salon — 4–6× a local shop, but the gap buys Japanese-language consultation, Japanese products and consistent technique.

Colour has the biggest price spread

From a ฿1,400 root touch-up to ฿3,000+ design colour. For high-tone colour with bleach, technique drives the result — choose on colour expertise (portfolio, a dedicated colourist) rather than price.

Premium Japanese treatments are available here

Japanese treatments like TOKIO are around ฿2,000 in Bangkok — sometimes cheaper than in Japan.

Salon-choosing checklist

  • Distance from the BTS (a 5–7 min walk matters in the rainy season)
  • Japanese / English support
  • Specialty (colour-focused vs cut-focused)
  • Whether Japanese products are used
  • Online booking (e.g. ระบบจองออนไลน์)
